Using Errand

Terms and Conditions

Effective September 28, 2026

Using the marketplace

Errand connects people who post local tasks with independent community members who offer to complete them. You must provide accurate information, use an account you control, and follow applicable laws when using the service.

Tasks and offers

Posters are responsible for clear task descriptions, realistic budgets, and safe arrangements. Runners are responsible for reviewing task details before making an offer. An agreement is formed when a poster accepts an offer; both people should confirm timing, location, and any relevant details before work begins.

Respectful and safe conduct

Harassment, discrimination, fraud, unsafe requests, and unlawful activity are not allowed. Do not post tasks involving prohibited goods or services. Errand may restrict access when conduct threatens the safety or integrity of the community.

Payments and task costs

The agreed runner fee and any task expenses should be clear before work starts. Posters and runners should agree in advance how purchases, reimbursements, cancellations, and changes will be handled. Do not share payment credentials or sensitive financial information in public task details or messages.

Independent participants

Runners choose which tasks to offer on and determine how they complete accepted tasks. They are not employees of Errand. Posters and runners are responsible for their own decisions and for complying with local requirements.

Service availability

Errand may update or change marketplace features to maintain the service. The platform is provided subject to applicable law, and availability may vary by location or over time.

Questions

For questions about these terms, contact Errand support.
