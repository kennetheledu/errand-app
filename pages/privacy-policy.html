Your information matters

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Information you provide

When using Errand, you may provide account details such as your name and email address, profile information, task descriptions, locations, messages, offers, and payment-related details. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public task descriptions.

How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, match posters with runners, communicate about errands, support payments and task records, help protect the community, and respond to support requests.

What other users can see

Task details you publish, your public profile, ratings, and offers may be visible to other Errand members. Contact details should be shared only when needed to complete an agreed task.

Sharing and protection

Errand does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the marketplace, when needed to complete a task, or when required to protect users or comply with law. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can review and update account information through your profile. You may request account closure or ask a privacy question by contacting Errand support.

Policy updates

This policy may be revised as Errand’s services change. The effective date above will be updated when a new version is posted.
